# 02 — The Competency-Based Approach (APC / CBA)

*Confidence legend in [`README.md`](README.md). Source URLs inline.*

## 1. What it is, and Cameroon's specific model

**APC** (Approche Par les Compétences) / **CBA** (Competency-Based Approach) replaced the older
**PPO / APO** (Pédagogie/Approche Par les Objectifs — Objective-Based). The shift: from
transmitting fragmented, decontextualized knowledge → to developing **competences** (the ability
to mobilize and combine resources to solve complex, real-life problems). Teacher = facilitator;
learner = central actor. ✅ ([IJHSSE](https://www.arcjournals.org/pdfs/ijhsse/v8-i1/11.pdf))

**Cameroon's declension: APC-ESV** — *"APC avec entrée par les situations de vie"* → **"CBA with
entry through real-life situations."** The real-life situation is the entry point for all
teaching. ✅ (verbatim in bilingual MINESEC syllabi)
- General secondary (ESG): entry through *situations de vie*.
- Technical (ESTP): entry through *situations de profession/métier*.

Theoretical source: **Xavier Roegiers' "pédagogie de l'intégration"** and the **BIEF/De Ketele**
methodology (integration, families of situations, criterion-based evaluation). ✅

**Macro-structure:** subjects grouped into **5 areas of learning / domaines d'apprentissage**
(Languages & Literature; Science & Technology; Social Sciences/Humanities; Personal Development;
Arts & National Cultures); content anchored in **domaines de vie / areas of life** (~5). ⚠️ exact
wording of the five life-domains varies between syllabi.

**Timeline:** founding law Loi n°98/004 (1998); **secondary rollout 2014/2015** (from 6ème/Form 1,
rolling up); **unified primary curriculum 2018/2019**. ✅ (arrêtés 263/14 & 419/14 circulate but
verify against primary docs ⚠️.)

**Reality check (set product expectations):** implementation is *incomplete* — teachers often
relabel old PPO objectives as "competencies," textbooks/training/infrastructure lag, many report
cards still grade PPO-style, large classes block individualized teaching. ⚠️ The app should
**support APC structure without forcing it** — meet teachers where they are.

## 3. The lesson — fiche de préparation & déroulement

⚠️ **No single legally-binding national fiche template.** Headers and phase names recur but vary
by subject, primary vs secondary, and ENS/inspectorate guide. Build a **flexible template with a
stable backbone**. (Concrete layouts in [`03-teacher-documents.md`](03-teacher-documents.md) §4.)

**Header / "page de garde" (recurrent fields):** Discipline · Durée · Module · Classe + Effectif ·
Nature/Titre de la leçon · Période (time slot) · Compétence visée/attendue (phrased as solving a
real-life situation) · Supports/Auxiliaire didactique · Situation problème / Corpus · (PPO variant
adds O.P.O + Prérequis). ✅ (from real ENS-Yaoundé filled fiches)

**Déroulement — phase models:**
- **Secondary 3-phase macro-model:** Introduction (discovery of situation problème + consignes) →
  Réalisation (treat individually/in groups) → Intégration/confrontation (present, justify, self-correct).
- **Common 6–7 step spine (flexible default):** Révision/mise en situation → Découverte →
  Analyse → Confrontation/validation → Généralisation/synthèse → Consolidation/application →
  Évaluation. Let the user collapse to 3 macro-phases or expand to ~7 steps.

**Body table (real Cameroon APC example):** `Étapes | Durée | Contenus | Supports | Activités
d'apprentissage` — optionally split *Activités enseignant | Activités apprenant*, add *OPI* or
*Traces écrites*. ✅

> ⚠️ Do **not** use the AUF "modèle de fiche APC" as Cameroon's standard — it's **Côte d'Ivoire**.
> Useful regional comparator only.

## 4. Assessment under APC (vs PPO)

PPO = atomized objectives graded right/wrong. APC = assess the **competence** via a complex
production against weighted **criteria + indicators**. ✅

**Three evaluation functions:**
- **Diagnostique** — check prerequisites before a unit.
- **Formative** — during learning, tied to **remediation** (correction et remédiation).
- **Sommative / certificative** — at the end of a *palier*, via resolution of a complex
  problem-situation. Cameroon stresses APC assessment is "more certificative than summative."

**The integration situation is the central assessment tool** (a *new* complex situation from a
known family, requiring integration of several resources — not a sum of isolated exercises). ✅

**Cameroon's operational exam format (well-attested):** a subject split into **two parts —
évaluation des ressources (≈9 pts) + évaluation de l'agir compétent (≈9 pts)** (+ presentation
pts). ✅ ([example](https://diotime.lafabriquephilosophique.be/numeros/090/013/))

**Criteria & indicators (Roegiers/BIEF apparatus Cameroon imports):** *critère minimal* (required
to certify) vs *critère de perfectionnement*; *indicateur* (observable); *grille de correction*
with a *barème*. The **2/3 rule** (minimal criterion mastered at ≥2/3) and **3/4 rule** (minimal
criteria ≥75% of marks) are ⚠️ **BIEF methodology, not confirmed in a Cameroonian regulation** —
offer as optional presets, don't hard-code as national rules.

## 5. Anglophone vs Francophone

**Same CBA framework, same designing body (MINESEC IGE), bilingual documents.** Differences are
language, certification exams (GCE vs BEPC/Probatoire/Bac), and minor terms — *not* a different
model. "module" = "unit" on the Anglophone side; the Anglophone "Scheme of Work (CBA)" mirrors the
Francophone "répartition annuelle / fiche de progression." ✅

> ⚠️ The claim that competency reform was "less systematic in anglophone countries" refers to
> Nigeria/Uganda/Swaziland — **not** Cameroon's Anglophone subsystem.

## 6. Content hierarchy (drives the data model)

```
Subject → Module(s)  (≈3–8 per subject/year, each with an hour CRÉDIT / "unit" on EN side)
        → Topic → Chapter → daily Lesson
```
Confirmed example — **Maths 6ème** (official MINESEC PDF, Aug 2014): annual volume 100h, 4h/week,
coefficient 4; 4 modules (32h / 11h / 46h / 11h). Humanities skew to 2–3 modules/year. ✅
([Maths 6e/5e programme](https://files.minesec.gov.cm/direct/view.php?s=2b&%2FPROGRAMME_MATH_6%C3%A8me_5%C3%A8me.pdf=))

> **Critical modeling nuance:** in the official *programme* the lowest grain is **catégories
> d'actions / ressources**, NOT "leçons." The chapter/lesson breakdown is derived by the
> teacher/department in the **répartition / fiche de progression** layer (see doc 03). Keep the
> immutable national syllabus separate from the teacher's local pacing.
